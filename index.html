The apartment

A recently renovated, spacious one-bedroom apartment with a separate living room and a terrace overlooking the garden and the mountains. Air-conditioned and fully equipped, it is a relaxed base for ski weeks, summer hikes and weekend escapes.

Guests can use the complex’s heated pool, sauna, steam room and fitness room. There is free private parking on site and free Wi-Fi throughout. The centre of Bansko, with the Holy Trinity Church, is about a 13-minute walk away.

The apartment

A spacious 78 m² apartment with two bedrooms and a living room, sleeping up to five guests. A stone fireplace, soundproofed windows and a balcony with river views make it especially cosy in winter, and the air conditioning keeps it comfortable in summer.

The kitchen is fully equipped, and the bathroom has a shower, bidet and hairdryer. Free private parking and a lift are available. The Holy Trinity Church is an 8-minute walk away, the Tourist Information Centre about 350 m, and the Gondola Lift roughly a 5-minute drive.
